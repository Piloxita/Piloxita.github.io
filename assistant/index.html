Piloxita Home Assistant

A private assistant that one household runs on its own computer. It helps the household's members with everyday office work: finding and summarising emails and documents, drafting replies, and preparing files.

It is not a public service. It has no sign-up and is used only by the people who set it up.

It asks for Gmail and Google Drive access only to do what the signed-in person asks: searching and summarising their email and files, saving drafts, and downloading files they request. Nothing is used for advertising, profiling or training; see the privacy policy.
